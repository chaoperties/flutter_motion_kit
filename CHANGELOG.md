## 0.1.0

* MotionButton with 11 effects, ported from Amicro.
* MotionButton `trigger: MotionButtonTrigger.press` for actions (Copy, Like, Save): the
  "Copied"-style state shows only after a click, never on hover. Hover stays the default
  for decorative effects.
* 20 loaders (dots, rings, bars, shapes, text), ported from Amicro.
* All 12 of Amicro's card layouts: CardArc (+ .seven, .long), CardLinearSpread, CardCornerFan,
  CardStampArc, CardCascadeStagger, CardScatterSpread, CardWheelFan, CardCarousel, CardCoverFlow,
  CardTimeMachine. Gallery page "Card Layouts".
* Gallery restyled in Amicro's design language (Outfit, neutral palette, light/dark toggle).
* MotionButton hover no longer depends on focus highlight mode; timers are cancelled on dispose.
